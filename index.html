About Me

With a deep-rooted passion for Hindustani classical music, I specialize in Dhrupad and voice culture, known for its profound depth and rich vocal tradition. Trained in the Dagarvani style, I have pursued formal education from Visva Bharati Santiniketan (B.A. & M.A.) and am currently pursuing a Ph.D. in Indian classical music from Barkatullah University, Bhopal. My musical journey includes guidance under esteemed gurus like Shri Murli Dhar Jagudi, Ma Aloka Nandy, and Shri Niloy Ahsan, along with time spent at Ustad Nasir Moinuddin Dhrupad Sangeet Ashram and with the Gundecha Brothers.
I offer individual and group classes in Hindustani Classical Vocal, with a focus on developing voice, understanding Raag, and mastering rhythm through Dhrupad and Dhamaar. With over 7.5 years of teaching experience, both online and offline, my goal is to help each student connect deeply with sound and tradition.

WHAT I TEACH

Everything you need to begin and grow in Hindustani classical vocal music

🌱 Foundational Training

What You’ll Learn: Our beginner-level training is thoughtfully designed to build a strong musical foundation while keeping the learning journey engaging and enjoyable.

  • Understanding Notes (Swar/Swar Sthan) - Learn the placement and significance of notes in the context of the Raag you’re studying
  • Introduction to Raag & Bandish - Begin exploring a Raag followed by a simple Bandish (song) to grasp its essence in a melodic and enjoyable way.
  • Basic Sargam Patterns (Alankaar/Palta) - Practice foundational sargam patterns to strengthen your vocal control and precision.
  • Elementary Rhythm & Taal Training - Get familiar with basic rhythmic concepts, practice sargam with rhythm, and explore simple Taals for better musical timing.

📅 Class Frequency: To ensure steady progress, students at the beginner level are encouraged to take at least two sessions per week. While three sessions are ideal, a minimum of two will help maintain consistency and retention.

🎶 Intermediate Level

What You’ll Learn: Designed for learners who already have a basic foundation in Hindustani classical music, the intermediate course takes your skills to the next level.

  • Refining Raags Already Known - Build on the raags you've previously learned with deeper exploration and enhanced application of Taal and Sargam techniques.
  • Enhanced Taal & Sargam Techniques - Progress is based on your previous experience and practice. Some students may require a few months to brush up past learning before moving ahead.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Learning any new skill—especially in the field of performing arts—requires time, patience, and consistent effort. Whether you're a complete beginner or revisiting music after a gap, the journey is personal and depends on how much time and focus you can dedicate. Once you grasp the foundational concepts and commit to regular practice, your learning path will naturally become clearer.

Class duration varies based on the learner’s level and the focus of the session. For absolute beginners, sessions may be around 40–45 minutes, primarily to train the ear and introduce basic concepts. As students progress, class durations may increase depending on the depth of material being covered and the learner’s engagement.

Yes, you can pay in INR. While fees for international students are listed in USD, those residing abroad can simply convert the amount to INR and make the payment. This provides flexibility for students across the globe.

There is no rigid course structure as each student is unique in terms of prior knowledge and learning pace. The teaching approach is personalized—tailored to your current level, goals, and progress. Whether you’re a beginner or have previous training, the learning plan will evolve accordingly.

I have had the privilege of teaching over 100 students so far—each with their own learning journey and musical goals.

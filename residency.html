Residency

✔ Duration of Stay:

  • Minimum Stay: 7 days
  • Maximum Stay: 30 days
  • You may choose your duration between 7 to 30 days based on your availability and learning goals.

✔ Facilities Provided:

We aim to make your stay comfortable and focused on learning. The following amenities are included:

  • A bed with clean bedsheets and pillows
  • Electricity and WiFi
  • Study table and chair
  • Dedicated space for daily riyaaz (practice)

✔ Food Arrangements:

  • Vegetarian meals will be provided daily.
  • Students preferring non-veg or other food may cook or order for themselves.
  • Children attending the residency will be served cooked food.
  • Adult students are encouraged to use the available kitchen space for simple meals.

✔ Residency Guidelines:

  • Wake-up time is 5:00 A.M. followed by guided riyaaz
  • Maintain cleanliness of your room
  • No alcohol consumption allowed
  • Lights out by 9:30 P.M.
  • Daily cleaning participation is encouraged (e.g., mopping, watering plants, dishwashing, etc.)
  • Other activities like yoga, gym are not provided but can be self-arranged

Residency Fee

  • 7-Day Stay: ₹6,500
  • 30-Day Stay: ₹25,000
  • Per Day: ₹1,000

Security Deposit: ₹3,000 (Refundable upon successful completion of stay. Deductions may apply for any damages.)

Online Class Rules

  • Rescheduling Limit: Maximum 2 classes per month for both student and teacher.
  • Validity for Rescheduling: Rescheduled classes must be completed within the same month. Delayed make-up will not hold.
  • Vacation/Gaps: For breaks lasting 2 weeks or more, students must notify the teacher at least 4–7 days in advance.
  • Notice for Rescheduling: Any class reschedule must be requested at least 24 hours prior. Last-minute cancellations will be counted as a conducted class.
  • Emergency Cancellations: Only genuine medical emergencies will be rescheduled.
  • Fee Submission: Fees must be paid in advance by the 5th of each month.
  • Class Package: Students are required to pay in advance for the slot(10 classes) each month.
  • Monthly Recording: Students must submit at least one practice recording per month to track progress.
  • Missed Class by Teacher: If the teacher misses a class without prior notice, they will reschedule the missed session and waive the fee for one class in the following month.
  • Missed Class by Student: If the student misses a class without notice, it will be considered a completed session.

Offline Class Rules

  • Monthly Commitment: Offline classes are structured on a monthly/class basis.
  • Advance Payment: Fees are to be paid in advance every month.
  • No Rescheduling: Missed offline classes will not be rescheduled, even in the case of teacher holidays (except Saturday and Sunday).
  • Consistent Fees: Fees remain fixed whether the student attends 3, 5, or 4 days a week.
  • Leave Notification: Students must inform the teacher in advance if they plan to be absent for any occasion.

Fee Revision Policy

(Applicable to both Online & Offline Students)

  • After completing full 1 year, fees will increase slightly for the next 3 consecutive years.
  • No increase will be made for the following 2 years.
  • Fee structure will be the same unless a new increase again in the fifth year, and so on.
